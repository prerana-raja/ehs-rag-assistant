# Hot Work Procedure (Fictional Sample)

## Scope
Hot work means welding, cutting, grinding, brazing or any activity that produces sparks, flames or heat capable of igniting flammable material.

## Before starting
Remove or cover combustible materials within 10 metres. Test the atmosphere for flammable gas and record the reading. Place a fire extinguisher at the work location. Assign a fire watch who stays for the whole job.

## After finishing
The fire watch remains in place for at least 60 minutes after hot work ends and checks the area for smouldering material before signing off.
