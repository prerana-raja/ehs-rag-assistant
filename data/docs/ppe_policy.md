# PPE Policy (Fictional Sample)

## Minimum PPE on site
Safety helmet, safety glasses, high-visibility vest, safety boots and gloves suitable for the task are mandatory in all operational areas.

## Additional PPE
Hearing protection is required where noise exceeds 85 dB(A). Face shields are required for grinding. Fall arrest harnesses are required for work at height above 2 metres.

## Inspection
Inspect PPE before each use. Damaged or expired equipment must be removed from service and replaced.
