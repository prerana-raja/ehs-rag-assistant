# Incident Reporting (Fictional Sample)

## What to report
Report all injuries, near misses, property damage, environmental releases and unsafe conditions. Near misses are reported even if nobody was hurt.

## Timelines
Tell your supervisor immediately. Enter the incident in the EHS reporting system within 24 hours. Serious injuries and environmental releases must also be escalated to the regional EHS manager within 2 hours.

## Investigation
The supervisor starts a basic investigation within 48 hours. Root causes and corrective actions are recorded with an owner and a due date. Lessons learned are shared with the team.
