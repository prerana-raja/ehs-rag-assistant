# Permit to Work (Fictional Sample)

## When a permit is required
A permit to work is required for non-routine or high-risk tasks: hot work, confined space entry, work at height above 2 metres, electrical work on live equipment, and excavation. Routine tasks covered by an approved standard procedure do not need a permit.

## Issuing and closing a permit
The permit is issued by the area authority after a joint site walkdown with the performing authority. The permit is valid for one shift only and must be displayed at the work location. At the end of the shift or when the job finishes, both authorities sign to close the permit and confirm the area is left safe.

## Suspending work
Work stops immediately if conditions change, an alarm sounds, or a stop work call is made. The permit is suspended and must be re-validated before restart.
