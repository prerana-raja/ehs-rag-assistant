# Lockout Tagout (Fictional Sample)

## Purpose
Lockout tagout (LOTO) prevents unexpected energisation or release of stored energy during maintenance.

## Steps
1. Notify affected personnel.
2. Shut down the equipment using normal stop controls.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal and gravity.
4. Apply a personal lock and tag to each isolation point. Each worker uses their own lock.
5. Release or restrain stored energy.
6. Verify zero energy by attempting a start and testing with an instrument.

## Removing locks
Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. If that person is unavailable, a supervisor follows the documented lock removal procedure after confirming the worker has left the area.
